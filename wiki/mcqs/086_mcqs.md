## Question 2 · Authored clinical vignette
A child develops fever, cough, coryza, and conjunctivitis. Small whitish lesions are seen on the buccal mucosa before a generalized rash spreads from the face downward. Which disease best fits?

A. Roseola
B. Rubella
C. Measles
D. Varicella

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

C. Measles

The respiratory prodrome, conjunctivitis, buccal Koplik spots, and descending rash support measles.
